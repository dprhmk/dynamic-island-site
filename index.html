FAQ

Does it work with Spotify / YouTube / any browser?

Yes. Dynamic Island reads the system-wide Now Playing session, so anything that shows up in macOS media controls — Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, VLC, podcasts in a browser tab — shows up in the island, with working play/pause, next/previous, and scrubbing.

Does my MacBook need a notch?

It's designed for notched MacBooks (2021 and later). On external displays it falls back to a compact floating island at the top of the screen.

Will it drain my battery?

No. At rest the app uses zero CPU — measured, not marketed. Even the music equalizer animation is offloaded to macOS's render server.

Where do my files and screenshots go?

Nowhere. The shelf is a temporary, in-memory pocket — files stay exactly where they are on disk, and the app never uploads anything. There is no network access, no analytics, no account.

What are the requirements?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later on Apple Silicon. First launch will ask for access to your screenshots folder — that's the only permission it needs.
